This request stays open for 6 hours

Whichever urgency you pick, the request closes on its own 6 hours after you post it and stops accepting responses. You'll see the countdown on the tracking screen, and you can post it again afterwards.

Donors see the blood group, units, urgency and roughly how far away they are — by push notification, or by SMS if they have no app. They never see the patient name, and they see your contact details only after they accept.
