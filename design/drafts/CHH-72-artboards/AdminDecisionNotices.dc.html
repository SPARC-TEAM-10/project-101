What a verification decision sends

US-CHH-001-03 says an approved or rejected facility “should receive a notification”, and a rejection carries the reason. Left is what the admin sees; right is what lands on the facility's phone. Toast copy keeps the verb of the action, per the UI standard.

No push or SMS is drawn for a suspension. A suspended account is signed out, so a push has nowhere to land — the person meets the block at sign-in instead (see the “Suspended sign-in” artboards). Whether an SMS should be sent as well is an open question for the PO.
